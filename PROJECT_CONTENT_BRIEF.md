## Fields needed per project

1. **Project title** — official name, e.g. "Reconstruction of X Road"
2. **Client** — official client name (government agency, company, private developer)
3. **Our role** — exact title, e.g. "Supervising Consultant", "Contractor", "Design Consultant"
4. **Location** — specific town/LGA, not just "Edo State"
5. **Year(s) / duration** — start and end (or "ongoing"), not just one year
6. **Discipline/category** — Civil Engineering / Geomatics / Environmental / etc.
7. **Markets tag** — e.g. "Transport", "Government & Public Sector", "Private Development", "Oil & Gas"
8. **Services performed** — short tags, e.g. "Topographic Survey", "Geotechnical Investigation"
9. **Scope** — 1-2 sentences on what we were hired to do
10. **Challenge** — what problem existed before we got involved
11. **Approach** — what we specifically did (not generic — the actual steps/method)
12. **Outcome** — the result, ideally with a real number (length in km, households served, etc.)
13. **Stats** — 2 short "big number" callouts if available (e.g. "2.4km", "6 months")
14. **Quote** (optional) — a real named person + title, or say "no quote" to drop it
15. **Photo/video captions** — one line per image/clip you want used, in your own words
